## Questions related to the conference

While we cannot answer all possible questions, we will try to cover the most important once here already.

For more specific questions, [contact us](/#you-have-questions-or-want-to-contribute) via Matrix.

### Tickets

* **Which ticket do I need to buy?**  
  Companies should buy the **Company ticket**, as that allows us to cover upcoming costs. It also enables us to provide travel grants and catering.  
  Students, active community members and contributors can pick the **Community ticket**, which will be free.
* **Where do I find my ticket?**  
  Once the order has been filled, you will receive an email, containing the invoice and a link to your order. This page will also contain a link to the ticket, after your payment has been registered.
* **My payment status is still pending**  
  Please pay your order. You will not receive your ticket otherwise.  
  If you have payed already, please contact us. We then can search for your payment.
* **Can I pay after the conference?**  
  No. This not possible.
* **I no longer need my ticket**  
  Please cancel your order, so others can attend instead.  
  You can cancel your order for free, up to 14 days before the conference starts, on your booking page.

### Event

* **Which days should I attend at?**  
  If you are new or only have limited time, you should attend the conference days.  
  For more details see [What to expect at the conference?](/#what-to-expect-at-the-conference) and [What to expect at the hackdays?](/#what-to-expect-at-the-hackdays).
* **Do I need a ticket?**  
  Yes. We need to organize and therefore need to know the number of participants in advance.

### Location

* **Can I bring my dog / pet?**  
  No.

### Miscellaneous

* **Will there be recordings?**  
  While we would like to offer recordings in the future, we currently don't have the equipment for these. We instead will provide notes, that will be taken during each session.
